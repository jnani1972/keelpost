# Action log

One line per action: every file written, approval item added, hand-off, decision and stop. Newest last. Add new lines at the bottom; existing lines stay untouched.

Format: `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM | <agent> | <what happened> | <file or ->`

The two lines below are samples. Delete them before your first run.
