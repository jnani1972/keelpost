# Video scripts

Formats and the beat-by-beat layout the designer uses for every video. Scripts reuse the words of an approved draft; they do not add new claims.
How to use: keep the formats you will actually make and delete the rest. Every script is a table of beats so the picture, the voice-over and the on-screen text can be checked line by line.

## Formats

| format | purpose | length | channels |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| quick tip | one useful fix or check | {{fill: e.g. 15 to 30 seconds}} | instagram, tiktok |
| behind the counter | show the work and the people (with consent) | {{fill: e.g. 30 to 60 seconds}} | instagram, facebook |
| how-to | a full walkthrough | {{fill: e.g. 2 to 5 minutes}} | youtube, blog |
| {{fill: your own}} | {{fill: ...}} | {{fill: ...}} | {{fill: ...}} |

## Script layout

```
title:
format:
channel:
source draft: work/YYYY-MM-DD-<channel>-<slug>.md
voice-over voice: <chosen voice or [OWNER PICKS]>
```

## Example (Harbor Lane Bikes, delete when done)

title: Two-minute brake check. Format: quick tip. Channel: instagram. Source draft: work/2026-11-04-instagram-winter-tune-up-checklist.md. Voice-over voice: [OWNER PICKS]

| beat | seconds | picture | voice-over | on-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0-3 | close-up, hand squeezing a brake lever | "Squeeze your brakes before you ride." | Brake check |
| 2 | 3-9 | lever stopping short of the bar | "If the lever touches the bar, the cable needs a tweak." | Lever hits bar? |
| 3 | 9-15 | pads against a wet rim | "Worn pads stop slower in the rain." | Check your pads |
| last | 15-20 | shop front | "Bring it in Saturday and we'll take a look." | Book a service |

## Rules

1. Every voice-over line and on-screen line is copied from the source draft.
2. No real person's face or voice unless an asset with written consent is registered.
3. The voice-over voice is the one in `brand/visuals/specs.md`, or the script says `[OWNER PICKS]` and cannot ship.
4. Music only from the asset register.
5. Captions follow `brand/visuals/specs.md`.
