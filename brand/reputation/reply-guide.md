# Review reply guide

How the reputation agent drafts replies to reviews. Replies to unhappy reviewers always go to the owner first; this guide decides what the draft says.
How to use: fill the next step for complaints first, since every negative reply needs it. By default a review of 3 stars or fewer counts as negative; change the line below if you want a different cut.

**Negative means:** {{fill: e.g. "3 stars or fewer, or any review that complains, whatever the stars"}}

> Example (Harbor Lane Bikes): 3 stars or fewer, plus any 4-star review that describes a problem.

## Rules for every reply

1. Thank the person without using their name: a reply that names someone always waits for the owner, which would stop thank-you replies from going out.
2. Answer the point they actually made. Do not reply to a different review.
3. Never admit fault, offer a refund, or promise a fix in public. Offer the next step below instead.
4. Never mention another customer, a staff member's full name, or any order detail.
5. Never argue, correct their memory, or ask them to change their review.
6. Keep it under {{fill: e.g. 80}} words.
7. Follow `settings/voice.md` and `brand/identity/tone-rules.md`.

**Next step for complaints:** {{fill: the exact private route you offer, e.g. "Please call the workshop on [number] and ask for the manager."}}

## Pattern per star band

| stars | aim | shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | say thanks | thank them; mention the one thing they praised; invite them back. No offer, no promise. |
| 4 | thanks, and notice the gap | thank them; mention what they liked; if they named a small issue, say you have noted it. |
| 3 | take the point seriously | thank them; name their main point in neutral words; offer the next step for complaints. |
| 1 to 2 | calm and private | thank them for telling you; say you are sorry their visit went that way; offer the next step for complaints. Nothing else. |
| no text | short thanks | one line of thanks for 4 to 5 stars; for 1 to 3 stars, the next step only. |

## Example (Harbor Lane Bikes, 2 stars, delete when done)

Review: "Waited a week for a puncture repair."
Draft: "Thanks for letting us know. We're sorry the wait was longer than you expected. Please call the workshop and ask for the manager, and we'll look into what happened."

## Special cases

| case | what to do |
| --- | --- |
| false, abusive or not a customer | draft nothing public; add an `URGENT` pending item with the review link and a private note for the owner |
| health, safety or legal threat | draft nothing public; `URGENT` pending item |
| names a staff member | reply without the name; `URGENT` pending item |
| same complaint seen before | say so in the report with the earlier review links; the owner may want to fix the cause |
