**reply** means draft a reply that goes through the approval gate. **watch** means list it in the report. **ignore** means list nothing and never reply.

## When a competitor promotion counts

All of these must be true:

1. The competitor is on the watch list in `brand/identity/positioning.md`.
2. The promotion is seen in a mention and confirmed by a second, linked source (their site, post or listing).
3. It reaches our audience or our topics: {{fill: e.g. same area, same service, or a keyword in brand/search/keywords.md}}.

> Example (Harbor Lane Bikes): Example Cycle Depot advertises a half-price winter service in the same postcode, seen in a local forum post and on their own site.
